# Home (portfolio, single page)

Scope: `/` is the whole portfolio as one scrolling street; `/about`, `/projects`, `/contact` redirect to its sections. Mode: Experience.

Audience: recruiters and hiring managers, 60–90 s, desktop or phone. Job: grasp who Subhadip is (Senior SWE, Team Lead, AWS serverless, 10,000+ user platforms), then download the resume or contact him. Proof: resume facts and metrics only. Constraints: light only, sound opt-in-friendly and mutable, Vercel Hobby (static), reduced-motion honoured, skimmable without playing.

Memorable moment: the shopfront sign board swinging on its hooks and the passport of stamps filling as boards are visited.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Career as a painted Bengal bazaar street: every section is a hand-lettered enamel shop board, lit as you pass and stamped when visited. Refuses the dark neon dev portfolio and the white card-grid template.

OWN-WORLD: Limewash blue-white wall ground; enamel boards in chrome yellow, vermilion, peacock teal and leaf green with black hand-lettering carrying a hard offset shadow; painted borders with corner rivets; brass hooks and chains; rubber-stamp ink marks in vermilion. Display: Yatra One (sign-painter Latin); body: a sturdy humanist sans.

STORY: The recruiter sees the name board, reads role + proof numbers, walks the street (Experience shops, Project stalls, Skills rack), collects stamps (XP), and ends at the Enquiry counter: resume download, email, WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width yellow shop sign hung on two chains, SUBHADIP DAS at monumental scale, sub-board "Senior Software Engineer · Team Lead"; under it a painted counter strip with 3 counter numbers; primary plaque "Download Resume", secondary "Enquire"; stamp passport + sound toggle fixed top right.

FORM: Bazaar Signboard, grounded list position 7, seed:9e08407bd45fcc5f. Signature interaction: boards swing on spring physics toward the cursor/scroll and get rubber-stamped on first visit, with a stamp thud sound.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
